# Anatomy Explorer v4.0.3

**Installing on a Mac.** The installer from 4.0.2 stopped with "Can't set «class kind» to """ (-10006) on its first
progress check. That is fixed: open `Install-Anatomy-Explorer-Mac.zip` and double-click **Install Anatomy Explorer**.

**Radiology.** Every case's 3D reference now shows what its scan shows. Angiograms show the opacified vessel tree on its
own instead of a solid organ or a whole body; CT and MRI sections are cut at the scan's level with the soft tissue
between the organs filled in, so there is no empty space around them; mammograms show a breast with its lesion or
calcifications; and the pathology a scan shows (a mass, clot, dissection, stone, bleed, fracture, effusion) is drawn
in 3D where it lies. The scan's numbered labels now appear with the same numbers on the 3D reference, and most cases
have more of them. Every case was then checked against its scan and corrected where the picture, a label or a lesion
was off.

**3D models run faster.** Models made of many parts (cardiac muscle, the kidney, the heart) did most of their work per
part on every frame. Parts that look alike are now drawn together, which cuts that work by about ten times on those
models; how they look is unchanged. On Retina screens the 3D viewer uses 4x instead of 8x multisampling, since the
screen's own resolution already smooths edges. The rendering setting is now called "Anti-aliasing".

**Model part names.** Parts that showed build labels ("Hero intercalated disc (disc 13)", "cardiomyocyte__003") have
plain names. Lessons that refer to the old names still find the parts.

**Histology.** The library is organised by organ system with plain English titles and rewritten descriptions; slides
that were filed under the wrong tissue moved, and duplicates and non-histology images were removed. Slides can show
numbered labels on the image (the Labels box).

**Updating on a Mac.** Updates run alongside the app you installed, so Finder kept showing the original version and icon.
Once an update has run, the app offers to replace the copy in Applications with it (once per version; "Don't ask
again" turns this off, and **Updates → Check for updates…** has the same button).

**Fixes.** A page opened right after start-up (a link, or a histology slide) no longer jumps back to the atlas. The heart
regulation lesson's adrenal medulla step uses the atlas, since the kidney model no longer includes the adrenal gland.
