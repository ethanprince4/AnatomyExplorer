# Layered Human v4.0.11

The application, installers, shortcuts and download helpers now use the Layered
Human name. The existing GitHub address and update identities are retained so
installed copies can continue updating, with their progress and preferences.

- Dissection layer captions keep a consistent size when selected, and the
  “Show only this layer” text stays smaller and fits beside the rule.
- Model cards show just the model name. Full descriptions remain in the detail
  panel.
- Opening and resetting a model fits it between the side controls. Grouped
  captions use the same readable names as the parts tree.
- Models open and reset to the full teaching model, including separate detail
  displays and supported cutaways, with the parts tree collapsed. The heart starts
  with its wall coverings, electrical system and support structures hidden.
- Skeletal muscle, stomach wall and duodenum open and reset without an extra
  cutaway. Muscle fibers and sarcomeres, and the stomach's exposed glands, face
  the viewer. Saved views, Settings and Menu sit centrally in the sidebar footer.
- Axillary Skin replaces Scalp in the model catalogue.
- Model previews use clearer specimen angles, with intact vessel walls and an
  oblique full female reproductive model. Breast skin is continuous around its
  teaching cutaway; the remaining anatomy is preserved.
- The model viewer keeps Views on the right and Cutaway in the bottom controls.
  Duplicate controls and oversized catalogue hover descriptions are removed.
- Selected-part descriptions size to their content. Details floats over the right
  reading area without moving the model or toolbar. Atlas Full entry toggles it;
  left controls stay visible, and its scrollbar matches Histology.
- English labels and descriptions use esophagus and esophageal consistently.
- Model cards are ordered by name A–Z and reuse scaled preview images while scrolling.
- View names use plain anatomical descriptions. Kidney cortex and column patches
  select and hide as two coherent structures; redundant lobe views are removed
  from the chooser while authored lesson references remain valid.
- The catalogue retains compressed NPZ models with their authored cameras, tissue settings,
  labels and teaching controls preserved. The supplied refined liver lobule,
  bladder wall, cardiac muscle, capillary bed, axillary skin and spleen replace
  their previous versions. The supplied axillary skin remains GLB, its original
  format; the other 39 models use their exact reviewed NPZ sources.
- The axillary skin has softer protruding dermal corners, a completed rear
  adipose block and round left-side passages for nerves and blood vessels.
  The exposed rear tissue corridors are continuous, with rounded walls.
  Its parts and groups have readable names while retaining their source keys.
- The spleen has a smoother trabecular junction at the arterial entry, a front
  catalogue preview, and its enlarged filtering schematic visible on opening
  and reset. The arterial transition is continuous, with open vascular passages.
  The upper trabecular seam is repaired; the lower slit is closed, with a short
  shaded crease still present beside the venous notch.

The original review sources remain preserved. Downloads are built and checked
on Windows and Apple silicon macOS before publication.
