# Anatomy Explorer v4.0.2

This replaces 4.0.0 and 4.0.1, which were withdrawn because of crashes on Macs.

**Mac crashes.** Lists and drop-down menus were cleared and rebuilt while they were on screen: the 3D Models list after
a model opened, search results while typing, the histology picker, the lesson and radiology lists and the model
function steps. On a Mac this could crash the app whenever anything that uses accessibility was running (a window
manager, a password manager, dictation). They are now updated in place.

**Find your way back.** Back and Forward buttons in the top bar return to where you were before: from a micrograph to
the model part you were reading about, from there to the lesson step you came from. The arrow next to Back lists the
recent places.

**3D models.** Every model's parts list is organised under plain headings (for the kidney: Kidney, Collecting system,
Blood vessels and Nephron, each opening into its sections). Selecting a part shows details about that part only: its
own description, clinical notes and histology, with the whole model's overview one click away. Parts that showed
technical notes now have proper descriptions. The kidney no longer includes the adrenal gland.

**On a Mac.** Shortcuts no longer clash with the system: Quiz is ⌥⌘Q (⌘Q quits), Histology ⌃⌘5, the workspaces
⌃⌘1–5, fullscreen ⌃⌘F, and the Delete key hides the selection. In histology and radiology images a two-finger swipe
pans and a pinch zooms. Text is no longer smaller than on Windows, and a borderless window can be dragged by the empty
part of the top bar.

**Installing on a Mac.** The download is now `Install-Anatomy-Explorer-Mac.zip`: open it and double-click
**Install Anatomy Explorer**, which downloads the app, checks it and puts it in Applications. The first time, macOS may
ask you to allow it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

**Updating.** Existing copies update through the app as usual; study progress, notes and settings are kept. Installing
a newer release over a copy that had already updated now runs the newer release.
