# wgpu port: known differences from the OpenGL renderer

Every place where the wgpu renderer's picture can differ from today's OpenGL renderer, with the reason and size.
Sizes are in 1/255 of the final 8-bit image unless stated. "Same GPU" means GL and wgpu both on the RTX 3080.

| Area | Difference | Size | Why it is accepted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Textured parts, anisotropic filtering (8x) | GL and Vulkan/Metal drivers implement anisotropic filtering differently | same GPU: mean 1.2, max 201 at grazing angles; with anisotropy off on both sides: mean 0.01, max 2.2 | Driver sampler behaviour, not our code; the same texture through plain WGSL `textureSample` shows the same gap. On a Mac both GL (layered on Metal) and wgpu use Apple's sampler. |
| Textured parts in the full frame, anisotropy off on both sides | small unexplained residual in the synthetic textured scene (tools/perf/gpu/b2_check.py `tex_aniso1`) after mip chain and LOD were matched | mean 0.108 at 320x200 (opaque), 0.023 ghosted; test cap 0.2 | Open item. No library model uses textures today; re-check when one does |
| Stripe pattern next to a cut plane | `MAIN_FS` discards clipped fragments before its `fwidth`, so GL's derivative there is undefined; wgpu computes it analytically | same GPU: up to 55 on a few stripe pixels beside the cut | GL's value is undefined behaviour (a latent bug in `app/viewer/shaders.py`); wgpu gives the intended result. |
| Noise ("mottle") across GPU vendors | `fract(sin(x) * 43758.5)` hashes depend on each vendor's `sin` precision | NVIDIA vs Intel: a different random pattern on noise-textured parts (mean about 27 on those parts only) | Same between GL and wgpu on one GPU; GL itself differs the same way between vendors. Re-check on a Mac (Metal fast-math `sin`). |
| Selection outlines at 1.5 px width across vendors | outline taps landing exactly on texel boundaries round differently | NVIDIA vs Intel: up to 77 on 0.05-0.13% of pixels | Same between GL and wgpu on one GPU (bit-identical on NVIDIA). |
| SSAO | single-sample threshold flips from floating-point arithmetic order | same GPU: 19 of 921,600 pixels before blur; after blur max 3, mean 0.004 | Arithmetic noise; reduced from 716 pixels by matching the driver's fused multiply-add. |
| Ghosted parts next to a cut plane | `OIT_FS` also discards clipped fragments before `surface()`'s derivatives (undefined in GL); wgpu defines them | same GPU: a few pixels beside the cut | Same latent GL bug as the opaque stripe case above. |
| Ghosted parts across vendors | Intel's resolved accumulation differs from NVIDIA's by about one 8-bit level on textured alpha-cut rims | UHD 770 vs GL on RTX 3080: mean 0.23, max 43 | wgpu on the RTX 3080 matches GL within 2/255 (mean 0.017); the gap is Intel hardware. |
| Thin features under MSAA (visibility buffer) | GL's pre-pass takes ONE sample at the pixel centre; the wgpu geometry resolve only knows the triangles that cover one of the 4 samples, so a sliver that contains the centre but no sample is background in id / normal / depth (and gets no AO there) | 4x, 2560x1600, id differs from GL on 0.18% of pixels (eyeball), 0.26% (kidney_nephron), 1.75% (pancreas, 71,552 px; mostly silhouettes of thin parts); colour: pancreas default 0.48% of pixels over 8/255, 83% of them within 1 px of an image edge | Picks do not use it: when the resolved buffers hold background at a picked pixel, `pick` re-draws the visibility pass single-sampled at that pixel centre (GL's exact rule): 160/160 picks agree on eyeball, kidney_nephron, pancreas, tongue_papillae (99.4% on one tongue state) |
| Edge pixels, shading position | GL's `v_wpos` is `centroid` for partly covered pixels; wgpu shades each distinct triangle at the pixel centre on the triangle's plane with extrapolated barycentrics (all other varyings are non-centroid in GL too) and weights it by its sample count | mean abs diff 0.006 to 0.17 over 15 reference images (eyeball, kidney_nephron, pancreas); max 126-237 on a handful of edge pixels | Sub-pixel placement of a lit edge; no change away from edges |
| Not yet in the wgpu frame | (1) caps (cut faces): logged once per renderer, the sections show the inside of the shells; pancreas cut_plane has 1,557 cap pixels (0.04%), mean 0.155. (2) alpha-cut textures: `WgpuRenderer.set_model` raises `NotImplementedError` and the Qt view (`app/ui/model_view.py make_viewport`) builds the OpenGL viewport instead (logged once). (3) culling is not wired into the frame yet (app/gpu/cull.py is standalone). Drawn and checked against GL (tests/gpu/test_b2.py, tools/perf/gpu/b2_check.py, synthetic scene tests/gpu/b2_scene.py; no library model has morph targets, animation, textures or alpha-cut): morph weights, procedural animation (all three modes), ghosted/translucent parts through OIT including morph and animation, and textured parts in the shaded resolve and in OIT | b2_check 320x200 mean abs diff: morph 0.011, anim 0.005, anim modes 0.002, ghosted+morph 0.004, ghosted+animation 0.003; textures with anisotropy off on both sides 0.108 (ghosted 0.023); textures with 8x anisotropy 0.88 at this size (the row 1 driver gap) | Caps are a separate port step; alpha-cut needs a texture-alpha discard in the visibility pass (one texture binding per draw there), refused until then so a wrong picture is never shown |
