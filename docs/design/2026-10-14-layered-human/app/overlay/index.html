The coronary circulation
2 of 7 · The left coronary artery

A short left main stem passes between the pulmonary trunk and the left auricle and divides almost at once.

The anterior interventricular branch (LAD) runs down the front of the septum to the apex, supplying the anterior wall of the left ventricle, the apex and the anterior two thirds of the interventricular septum, including the bundle branches. The circumflex branch curls round in the left atrioventricular groove to supply the lateral and posterior left ventricle.

Clinical note
The LAD is sometimes called the “widow-maker”: it supplies the largest territory, and its proximal occlusion takes out the anterior wall and the conducting system together.
