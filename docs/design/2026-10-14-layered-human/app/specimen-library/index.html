The left coronary artery

Step 2 of 7 · The coronary circulation · Advanced

A short left main stem passes between the pulmonary trunk and the left auricle and divides almost at once.

The anterior interventricular branch runs down the front of the septum to the apex. It supplies the anterior wall of the left ventricle, the apex and the anterior two thirds of the interventricular septum, including the bundle branches. The circumflex branch curls round in the left atrioventricular groove to the lateral and posterior left ventricle.

Clinical. The anterior interventricular branch (LAD) supplies the largest territory; its proximal occlusion takes out the anterior wall and the conducting system together.
