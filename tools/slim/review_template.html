Model Slimming Review

Simplified copies of the heaviest parts of each 3D model, drawn by the app's own renderer from the same camera as the original. A copy was only made where its surface stays within a quarter of an edge length of the original and its normals stay within 0.8° of the original surface's own noise. Nothing is replaced until you choose Use copy; the originals are untouched.

Choices are not saved in this view.

Not simplified

These parts did not pass the checks even at half their triangles, so they keep their full mesh. Most are rough or folded surfaces where the original's own normals vary a lot from point to point.
